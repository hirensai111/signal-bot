Trend breakout v0.2.0

Donchian breakout on 4h bars, only in the direction of the 200-bar EMA and only on above-average volume: 2 ATR initial stop, 3 ATR constant-distance trailing stop (Delta bracket_trail_amount), no fixed target. Chosen on 2021-01..2025-09 development data as the centre of a stable parameter plateau.

Limitations

  • Prices and volumes are Binance USD-M futures; live execution is on Delta Exchange India, whose basis, depth and fees differ. The paper broker measures that gap on live Delta books.
  • Fees assume a 5 bps taker and 2 bps maker schedule and a constant funding rate; real funding varies and can be large in trends.
  • Bars hide the order of the high and low: when a stop and a target fall in the same bar the stop is assumed first, which understates results for wide-range bars.
  • One 12-month period. The out-of-sample window is its last third, so it covers a single regime.
  • One position per symbol at a time, as in live trading: signals arriving while a position is open are skipped.
